# Project background

The historical experiment was part of the CSULA RoboSub team's computer-vision work. The notebook records YOLOv7 training and validation for underwater object detection.

The detector implementation is [WongKinYiu/YOLOv7](https://github.com/WongKinYiu/yolov7), installed separately. Its source and weights remain subject to their respective licenses. This repository contains the surrounding dataset, evaluation, and run-management tools.
